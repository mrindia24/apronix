## What the device mockups show

The homepage hero, the Shopify and WordPress service images, and their matching
project covers are **composites**: a licensed photograph with a screenshot of
this website mapped onto the device screen with correct perspective, then graded
to sit inside the dark brand palette. The interface on those screens is our own
design — no third-party client work, no invented dashboards, and no fabricated
results are shown.

Screenshots were captured from the running site at 1440×900 (desktop) and
390×844 (mobile) and perspective-mapped with a homography, so the screen edges
follow the photographed device exactly.

## If you replace these images

- Prefer your own photography of the real team and workspace — it outperforms
  stock every time.
- Keep photography licensed for commercial use and record the source in this
  file.
- Portfolio covers must depict **authorised, real work** with client permission.
  Until then the sample projects stay flagged `isSampleContent` and display a
  "Sample" badge.
- Export covers at 1600 px wide, 16:10 for projects and 16:9 for articles, under
  ~250 KB.
